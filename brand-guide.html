Internal Reference

Brand Guide

This is the visual identity reference for the Murph Family Connection event companion site. Every color below was sampled directly from a photo of the official 2026 event shirt ("Stronger Together") and lightly calibrated for on-screen contrast and accessibility. Typography, section rhythm, and component styling were adapted from the storytelling polish of the murph_report_v3_1.html design reference. Neither source's artwork is reproduced anywhere on the site.

Using the Shirt as Inspiration

Do

Sample colors directly from the shirt photo. Borrow its general warmth — tree/roots motif, gold accents, rounded natural shapes. Use "Stronger Together" as text, since it's the event's own tagline.

Do not

Trace, crop, or otherwise reproduce the shirt's printed artwork (the tree badge, the banner graphic, the circular seal) anywhere on the site.

Map Markers

Numbered circles with a gold ring, drawn with Leaflet over a light basemap. Fill color signals category; the count is always shown as text, and every marker exposes a popup with the full community/county/state/count — color is never the only way information is conveyed. The map frame itself carries a 2px Royal Blue border with a 4px gold top accent.

Navigation

Exactly three items, always in this order: Home, Attendee Map, Family Tree Questionnaire. The header bar is navy (Royal Blue Dark) with a 3px gold bottom border and an ivory-backed logo mark; the active page shows a light overlay with a gold underline (see the header above). No dropdowns, no sidebar, no additional items — this is intentional and should not expand without a scope change. At three items the nav simply wraps on narrow screens rather than needing a hamburger menu. Every page also opens with the same navy page-header band as the hero, so the site feels like one connected companion rather than three disconnected pages.

Accessibility Requirements

  • ✅All text/background pairings meet WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 normal text, 3:1 large text/headings) — verified programmatically.
  • ⌨️Every interactive element (nav, buttons, map markers) has a visible focus outline and is reachable by keyboard alone.
  • 🎨Map marker color is a reinforcement, never the only signal — counts are shown as text, and popups spell out community/state/count.
  • 🌀prefers-reduced-motion disables hover-lift transforms, scroll-smoothing, and map pan/zoom animation.

Design Principles

Simple over clever

Two jobs, done well: explore the attendee map, complete the family tree questionnaire. Resist adding features beyond that scope.

Inspired, not copied

Colors and motifs are drawn from the event shirt. The shirt artwork itself is never reproduced or used as a background.

Warm, not corporate

Rounded cards, serif headings, and a confident navy-and-gold palette over gray dashboards or antique manuscript styling.

Data-driven, never hardcoded

Every number on the site is generated from data/attendees.json, so updating one file updates the whole site.
